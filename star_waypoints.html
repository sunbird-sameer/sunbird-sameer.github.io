Gimbal Star Waypoint Calculator

Point your gimbal wherever you want, note the az/alt from a compass/level app and the time, enter your location — this projects that fixed sky point forward and gives you pan/tilt offsets for each waypoint. No camera, no star lookup needed.

Pan offset = azimuth change from waypoint 0 (+ = move right/clockwise). Tilt offset = altitude change from waypoint 0 (+ = move up). Az/Alt rates are not linear over long spans, especially near the horizon or celestial pole — if consecutive tilt/pan jumps look large, increase the number of waypoints rather than trusting interpolation between two widely spaced points.
